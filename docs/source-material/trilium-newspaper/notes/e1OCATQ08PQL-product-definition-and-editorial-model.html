Product Definition and Editorial Model

Status: Current long-term product definition for the personalized Newspaper expansion. This is a planning and product-specification subtree, not an implementation-progress log.

Purpose

The mature Newspaper App combines two first-class products over one shared ingestion and article system:

  • Reading Feed: the article-centric RSS-style reading product already being completed in the repository.
  • Newspaper: a stable daily edition that clusters multi-source coverage into neutral, provenance-aware newspaper articles.

The goal is not merely AI summaries. It is a personal news-intelligence system that reconstructs useful reality from fragmented, repetitive, biased, manipulative, and differently reliable sources while keeping important factual claims traceable.

Canonical Product Notes

  1. Product Experience and Edition Contract — Reading Feed versus Newspaper, morning delivery, front page, full articles, stable editions, automatic publication, and variable edition length.
  2. Source Configuration, Admission, and Ranking — per-output routing, source/topic weights, singleton behavior, weighted admission, prominence, breadth, evidence independence, and alternative sources.
  3. Editorial Synthesis, Evidence, and Corrections — neutrality, claim roles, scoped primary-source authority, interested-party handling, uncertainty, citations, corrections, and retractions.
  4. Story, Event, Timeline, and Section Model — continuing stories, discrete events, claim/development timelines, daily articles, Story So Far, event/report times, controlled sections, placement, and tags.
  5. Product Decision Ledger and Open Questions — concise settled-decision ledger, remaining product decisions, and the repository handoff boundary.
  6. Outlet and Upstream Processing Domain Model — explicit Outlet, Input Feed, Output Feed, Article Appearance, outlet-wide dedupe, app-owned classification, upstream processing, and the Intake Group migration.

Current Product Contract

  • The canonical Newspaper is a hosted, immutable edition. PDF is deferred vanity/export polish.
  • Email is a lightweight delivery envelope with the date, section availability, lead headlines, and one edition link.
  • Content cutoff and delivery deadline are independently configurable. Delivery is a hard deadline.
  • One or more valid stories publish automatically; zero stories produce a failed run and failure notification.
  • Generation is deterministic from a sealed manifest with no mutable editorial draft.
  • Generation and phase-level duration metrics support schedule tuning.
  • Outlet is the real-world publishing identity, dedupe boundary, Newspaper source-vote unit, and policy scope.
  • Input Feeds are discovery endpoints; Output Feeds group reading content; Article Appearances record discovery.
  • Extraction and classification occur upstream of outputs against the shared article pool.
  • Weights are absolute effective multipliers centered on 1.0, with visible inheritance and overrides.
  • Ordinary nonlocal admission starts at two eligible Outlets and combined weight 2.0; Local and primary-evidence exceptions are explicit.
  • Coverage breadth determines attention; evidentiary independence determines claim confidence.
  • Primary and interested-party sources are authoritative only within the scope of what they actually establish.
  • The main story experience is readable synthesized prose with numbered citations for unique and consequential claims.
  • Continuing stories use a minimal Active/Dormant lifecycle, discrete events, temporal claims, and an optional concise Story So Far.
  • The Newspaper uses an extensive, evolvable controlled taxonomy, one primary placement, and secondary tags; Politics is a tag.
  • Local is a geographic policy family with place-aware relevance and a bounded high-weight singleton exception.
  • Published errors are handled through linked corrections, clarifications, and retractions rather than silent rewrites.
